        What do these metrics mean?
        Total Sales

        All Amazon sales during the current reporting period.

        Ad Sales

        Sales Amazon attributes to advertising.

        Ad Spend

        Amount spent on Amazon advertising.

        ROAS

        Advertising sales generated for every $1 spent. Higher is generally better.

        TACOS

        Ad spend divided by total sales. It shows how advertising investment relates to the whole business.

        Conversion Rate

        The percentage of product-page sessions that result in an order.
